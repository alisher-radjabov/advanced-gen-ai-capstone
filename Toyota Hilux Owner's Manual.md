# Toyota Hilux Owner's Manual

## Chapter 1: Getting Started

### Engine Specifications
The Toyota Hilux comes with a 2.8L turbo diesel engine that delivers excellent performance and fuel efficiency. The engine produces 204 horsepower and 500 Nm of torque.

### Starting the Vehicle
To start your Toyota Hilux:
1. Insert the key into the ignition
2. Press the brake pedal
3. Turn the key to the START position
4. Release the key once the engine starts

### Dashboard Warning Lights
The dashboard contains several warning lights that indicate the status of various systems:
- Engine warning light: Indicates engine problems
- Oil pressure light: Low oil pressure warning
- Battery light: Charging system malfunction
- Temperature gauge: Engine overheating warning

## Chapter 2: Maintenance

### Regular Service Intervals
Your Toyota Hilux requires regular maintenance to ensure optimal performance:
- Oil change: Every 10,000 km or 6 months
- Air filter replacement: Every 20,000 km
- Brake inspection: Every 20,000 km
- Tire rotation: Every 10,000 km

### Oil Change Procedure
To change the engine oil:
1. Warm up the engine to operating temperature
2. Turn off the engine and wait 5 minutes
3. Remove the drain plug and drain old oil
4. Replace the oil filter
5. Install new drain plug with new gasket
6. Add new engine oil (6.1 liters for 2.8L engine)
7. Check oil level with dipstick

### Tire Pressure
Maintain proper tire pressure for safety and fuel efficiency:
- Front tires: 35 PSI
- Rear tires: 35 PSI (unloaded) / 40 PSI (loaded)
- Spare tire: 60 PSI

## Chapter 3: Troubleshooting

### Engine Won't Start
If your engine won't start, check the following:
1. Battery charge level
2. Fuel level
3. Starter motor operation
4. Ignition system

### Overheating Issues
If the engine overheats:
1. Stop the vehicle safely
2. Turn off the engine
3. Check coolant level (when cool)
4. Check for leaks in the cooling system
5. Contact Toyota service center if problem persists

### Electrical Problems
Common electrical issues include:
- Dead battery: Check battery terminals and charge level
- Blown fuses: Check fuse box and replace blown fuses
- Faulty alternator: Have charging system tested

## Chapter 4: Warranty Information

### Standard Warranty
Toyota Hilux comes with:
- 3 years or 100,000 km comprehensive warranty
- 5 years or 130,000 km powertrain warranty
- 12 years anti-corrosion warranty

### Warranty Coverage
The warranty covers:
- Manufacturing defects
- Powertrain components
- Electrical systems
- Air conditioning system

Warranty does not cover:
- Normal wear and tear
- Damage from accidents
- Modifications not approved by Toyota
- Damage from improper maintenance
